About

Seventeen. Building anyway.

No permissions asked. No shortcut taken. Just products shipped.

I'm a 17-year-old founder and developer from Harare, Zimbabwe. I started building in early 2026 and haven't stopped. Three working products. All shipped between school terms.

My focus is the informal economy — the traders, vendors, and small business owners who power Zimbabwe's real economy yet are completely ignored by modern financial technology. My current flagship product, GekoBooks, lets them track profit, inventory, and build a real credit identity using nothing but WhatsApp.

I was the top student in Zimbabwe for Cambridge IGCSE English Literature. I won the national BancABC Money Week quiz. I'm predicted A* A* A at A-Level in Accounting, Business, and IT.

I'm heading toward ACCA, then CISA, then CFA — building the qualifications to back the products I make.

GekoBooks
WhatsApp Fintech for Informal Traders

A WhatsApp-based bookkeeping platform that helps Zimbabwe's informal traders record purchases, sales, expenses, and loans — all by sending simple messages. Automatically tracks profit, balance, inventory, and generates AI-driven business insights. Includes a credit-scoring engine for micro-lenders and a web dashboard.
